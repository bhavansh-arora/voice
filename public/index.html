First time here? Set up an agent in four steps:
  1. Create an agent below (any name - just for you to recognize it).
  2. Open Setup and describe your business in plain language - it fills in the opening line, how it describes itself, and what to collect from callers.
  3. Open Voices to preview and pick a Hindi and English voice.
  4. Open Test to have an actual spoken conversation with it from your browser - no phone needed. When you're happy, flip it to active on the Setup page and set up Telephony to take real calls.
